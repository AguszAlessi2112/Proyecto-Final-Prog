ABOUT ME

My Name Is John Taylor

I’m John Taylor, one of the top 10 photography and filmmaking artists living in Florida. The way to my passion was long – filmmaking, branding, painting, and many other exciting things. However, my digital photo manipulation and compositing turned into my own style made it possible for me to work in several areas including journalism, nature photography, art nude portraits, and others. My photography collections take part in multiple festivals and contests. So, I’m sure you’ll love your photos! I love sharing my artworks and happy to say that it’s an excellent way to receive feedback and develop yourself. I’m also a leading fashion and travel photographer.
